- The app opens on your projects, not a list of sessions: each with its icon and colour, how many of its sessions need an answer, how many are at work, and the one it would open on. The space is picked from the title, and what is pinned comes first. With no computer that keeps a workspace, the projects are the folders, as before.
- A project opens as a browser does, on a tab: the session that waits for you, else the one you looked at last. The bar at the bottom goes back, shows the tab and how many there are, and opens a new one, a new session in that project; swiping the bar's tab goes to the next. The tabs are the computer's sessions, so none is closed from the phone but by stopping it, which asks first.
- The tab overview shows a project's sessions as cards, each with its state and what it last said or waits on, and under them the panels that go with the project.
- What needs an answer, in any space, is at the top of the first screen, an approval allowed or denied where it stands.
- Panels are listed by their titles, and a block's charts are drawn: lines, bars and sparklines.
- When no computer can be reached the app says so, and offers to try again.
- The colours are the desktop's greys, light and dark, so that a project's colour and a session's state are what stand out.

Panel titles and charts need the computer's mako to be of this release as well.
